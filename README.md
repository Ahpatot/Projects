A little simulation I created after the inspiration I got while playing noita. The best and most different feature in this smulation/game is the growing plants in my opinion. 

You can select the type of pixels by the extra window or just the key bindings given on them. You can hold down left mouse button to place a bunch depending on the size you adjust with the mouse scroll, or just place one pixel with right mouse button. Also you can see the type of pixel you have selected and type of pixel your mouse is currently on in the simulation window's name.
